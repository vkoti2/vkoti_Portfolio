01 / About

Curiosity, structure,
and a user-first mindset.

I’m a Technical Writer with 8+ years of experience across healthcare documentation, SaaS product content, SOPs, process writing, and information architecture.

I work closely with product, engineering, QA, business teams, and subject matter experts to understand workflows, identify gaps, and create documentation that supports both users and internal teams. My focus is on translating complex systems into clear, accurate, and actionable content.

Across roles in eClinical SaaS, US healthcare operations, and documentation operations, I have created user guides, troubleshooting content, release communication, process documentation, FAQs, and knowledge base material designed for real-world use.

I enjoy building documentation that is not just informative, but maintainable, consistent, and user-centered — especially in regulated or detail-heavy environments.
